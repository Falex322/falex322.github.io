Block 5 — FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Pricing & subscription
How does the free trial work? ▾
When you download Dzeny, you get 7 days of full access to all features — AI therapy sessions, mood tracking, personalized practices, and more. No credit card is required to start. After 7 days, you can choose a paid plan or continue with limited access.
What happens after the free trial? ▾
After your 7-day trial ends, you'll be prompted to choose a Monthly or Yearly plan. If you don't subscribe, you'll lose access to premium features but can still explore limited functionality.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Yes. Dzeny subscriptions have no long-term commitment. You can cancel at any time through your App Store or Google Play settings. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.
What payment methods do you accept? ▾
All payments are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Dzeny does not store your payment information directly.
Is there a refund policy? ▾
Refund policies are governed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store terms. Please contact Apple or Google support for refund requests.
Can I switch between plans? ▾
Yes, you can switch between Monthly and Yearly plans at any time through your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
Is my payment information secure? ▾
Absolutely. All transactions are handled by Apple or Google. Dzeny never has access to your credit card or payment details. Your conversations and data are 100% confidential.
