Questions

What is Dzeny? Is it a robot or a human?+
Dzeny is an AI-powered service designed to provide emotional support anytime, anywhere. It responds to your current feelings, analyzes them, and offers personalized exercises and a plan to help you build mental resilience. Dzeny is fully AI-based — not a human — but built with deep expertise from licensed psychologists and medical doctors.
Who can benefit from Dzeny, and in what situations?+
Dzeny is designed for anyone aged 18+ looking to improve their emotional wellbeing — students, office workers, expats, young parents, and anyone dealing with stress, anxiety, or low mood. If you simply want to build better mental health habits, Dzeny is for you.
How and why does Dzeny work?+
Behind Dzeny is a team of psychologists, medical doctors, and engineers who have integrated conversational AI, CBT, and APA best practices. Dzeny engages in meaningful dialogue to understand your thoughts and emotions, then recommends personalized practices and adapts over time.
Are my personal data and conversations secure?+
All conversations with Dzeny are 100% confidential and will never be shared, even in anonymized form. We will never sell or share user data for advertising or marketing purposes. Protecting your privacy is our top priority.
Can Dzeny replace therapy or sessions with a psychologist?+
No. Dzeny is not a substitute for therapy or clinical medical care. It's a valuable complement — whether you're just starting your mental health journey or looking for support between sessions with a professional. For crisis situations, please seek immediate professional help.
How is Dzeny different from BetterHelp or Talkspace?+
BetterHelp and Talkspace connect you with human therapists ($65–100/week). Dzeny is fully AI-powered — available 24/7, significantly more affordable, completely anonymous, and accessible without scheduling. It's your daily mental health companion.
What should I do if I'm in a mental health crisis?+
Dzeny is not designed for crisis situations. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — USA, free, 24/7), call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room.
