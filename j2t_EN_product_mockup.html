About Forex Trading

What is Forex trading and how does it work?

How does the forex market function?

The forex (Foreign Exchange) market is the world's largest and most liquid financial market, with a daily turnover exceeding $6.6 trillion. Currency prices are driven by global supply and demand, shaped by interest rate decisions from the Fed, ECB and Bank of England, inflation data, employment reports and geopolitical events.

How does trading EUR/USD work with Just2Trade?

When you trade EUR/USD on the J2T platform, you simultaneously buy euros and sell US dollars. If the euro strengthens, your long position gains value. Access is via MT4 or MT5 — available on desktop and mobile. For global (non-EU) clients, leverage of up to 1:500 is available. Read: MT4 vs MT5 — which is right for you? →

Why Just2Trade stands out for global traders

J2T maintains full MT4 access for all clients, offers CQG for futures and provides real (non-CFD) stock trading via Sterling Trader Pro — a combination unavailable at most retail brokers.
