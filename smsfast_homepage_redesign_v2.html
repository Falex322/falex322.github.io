What are temporary phone numbers?

Unlike free public numbers, SMSFAST assigns each number exclusively to one user — your codes stay private. Numbers are attached to real physical SIM cards, ensuring maximum compatibility with WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, and 500+ other services.
