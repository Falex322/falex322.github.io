Payment questions, answered fast
What happens if the SMS doesn't arrive after I buy a number?▼
The number's cost returns to your balance automatically. Click Cancel in your dashboard, and the refund posts within seconds. No support ticket is required.
What payment methods can I use to buy a number?▼
SMSFAST accepts Visa, Mastercard, cryptocurrency, and Alipay. Every method processes in real time, so your balance updates before you pick a number.
Do I need to create an account before I buy a number?▼
No separate sign-up is required. SMSFAST creates your account automatically the first time you buy, using the email or social login you provide at checkout.
Is there a monthly subscription, or do I pay per number?▼
SMSFAST charges per number, starting from $0.21, with no monthly subscription. You only pay again when you buy another number.
Can I buy a number from a specific country?▼
Yes. SMSFAST lists virtual numbers from over 190 countries, and prices vary by country and by service, so you can compare before you buy.
Is my number private, or can other people see the code?▼
A number bought on this page is private to your account. Unlike free, shared numbers, the code goes only to your SMSFAST dashboard, not to a public page other users can see.
Can I use the number I bought more than once?▼
That depends on the mode you choose. Activation mode gives one-time access for 20 to 90 minutes, while long-term rental keeps the number exclusive to you for 1 day up to 1 year.
