About Dr. Sarah Johnson

Dr. Sarah Johnson earned her Ph.D. in Clinical Psychology from Stanford University in 2012, specializing in anxiety disorders and cognitive behavioral interventions. Her doctoral research focused on the efficacy of digital mental health tools in treating generalized anxiety disorder, positioning her at the forefront of the digital mental health revolution.

Throughout her career, Dr. Johnson has treated over 1,000 clients struggling with anxiety, depression, and trauma-related conditions. She's passionate about making mental health care more accessible through technology while maintaining the highest standards of clinical practice.

Her therapeutic approach combines evidence-based cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) with mindfulness-based interventions, creating personalized treatment plans that address both immediate symptoms and long-term wellness goals. Dr. Johnson believes in empowering clients with practical tools and strategies they can use in their daily lives.

As a content contributor to Dzeny, Dr. Johnson applies her clinical expertise to create evidence-based, actionable mental health guidance. Her articles demystify complex psychological concepts and provide readers with scientifically-supported strategies for managing anxiety, stress, and emotional challenges.
