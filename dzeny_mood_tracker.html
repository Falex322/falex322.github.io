Overview

What Is Mood Tracking?

Mood tracking is the practice of regularly logging how you feel — and the thoughts, events, or habits connected to those feelings. Over time, it reveals patterns you'd never notice in the moment.

When you can see your emotional history clearly, you gain real power: you start to understand what lifts your mood, what drains it, and how to make small daily choices that add up to meaningful change.

Dzeny's AI-powered Mood Tracker goes beyond simple logging — it analyzes your data, surfaces hidden connections, and delivers personalized insights after every check-in.
