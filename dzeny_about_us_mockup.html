Блок 1 — Company Story
Our story

Dzeny was founded in [year] with a simple but powerful belief: mental health support should be available to everyone, anytime. What started as a small team of psychologists and engineers has grown into a trusted AI wellness platform used by thousands of people worldwide.

Our founders saw a gap between the growing demand for mental health support and the limited availability of affordable, accessible care. By combining clinical expertise with cutting-edge AI technology, we set out to create a tool that could offer meaningful, personalized support — day or night, wherever you are.

Since [year], Dzeny has been helping people navigate anxiety, stress, burnout, and everyday emotional challenges through AI-powered conversations shaped by insights from thousands of real therapy sessions.
